The Gilded Ledger

Merchants, rare materials & buyers for your loot

⚖ Market Rules

🔮 Buying magic items & crafting materials

  • Only in cities of the Kingdom. Searching takes at least 7 days and 100 GP.
  • Only Common and Uncommon items are sold on the Material Plane. Rarer items need another plane: at least 14 days and 200 GP.
  • Charisma (Persuasion) vs DC: Common 10 · Uncommon 15 · Rare 20 · Very Rare 25 · Legendary 30.
  • +1 for every extra 7 days and +1 for every extra 100 GP, up to +10.
  • Price per the Sane Magic Market table plus the base mundane item. Consumables cost half.
  • Crafting materials follow the same rule (DC by the rarity of the item to craft) and must be bought before crafting.
  • On a failure, you can't search for the same item again for 30 days.

💎 Selling magic items

  • Buyer search: Persuasion or Investigation vs the rarity DC. Once per day.
  • Negotiation: Persuasion or Deception vs the rarity DC. Success = full value, failure = half, natural 20 = 150%.

⚒️ Selling mundane items & materials

  • Mundane items: no search needed. Persuasion or Deception vs DC 13. Success = full value, failure = half, natural 20 = 150%.
  • Mundane raw materials sell for 100%. Beat DC 15 to sell for 150%. A natural 20 adds nothing extra.
  • The DM may step in for cursed, unique or restricted goods.
